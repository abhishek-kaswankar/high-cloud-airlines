# ✈️ High Cloud Airlines Data Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This data analytics project involves a comprehensive evaluation of High Cloud Airlines operations using a dataset of 110,849 flight records spanning 6 complete years (2008–2013)[cite: 6]. The objective is to analyze time intelligence, load factors, routing strategies, and carrier performance to improve airline profitability and operational efficiency[cite: 6].

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Analyzed
1. **Calendar Table & Time Intelligence:** Built a robust 11-field date spine for daily granularity, tracking weekdays, months, and financial quarters[cite: 6].
2. **Load Factor Analysis:** Tracked yearly, quarterly, and monthly load factors. Identified a 2.82 pp growth over 6 years, with a seasonal peak in July (81.19%) and a low in January (69.80%)[cite: 6].
3. **Carrier-Wise Load Factor:** Analyzed 209 unique carriers. Highlighted top performers like Globespan Airways (94.76%) and Allegiant Air (89.36%)[cite: 6].
4. **Top 10 Carriers by Passengers:** Evaluated market concentration, revealing that the top 4 carriers command ~49% of the 187M+ total passengers[cite: 6].
5. **Top Routes by Flights:** Identified key travel corridors (e.g., Chicago-Detroit, Washington-New York) driving network utilization[cite: 6].
6. **Weekend vs. Weekday Load Factor:** Compared occupancy rates to understand business vs. leisure travel demand[cite: 6].
7. **Flights by Distance Group:** Discovered that short-haul travel (Distance Group 1 with 58,047 flights) forms the core engine of operations[cite: 6].

## 💡 Key Business Strategies & Recommendations
- **Capacity Planning:** Reduce capacity during Q1 troughs (January) and expand inventory during the highly profitable Q3 peak (80.40%)[cite: 6].
- **Market Positioning:** With Southwest Airlines leading at 18.24% market share (34.1M passengers), High Cloud Airlines should target underserved corridors and pursue regional codeshares for network scale[cite: 6].
- **Dynamic Pricing & Fleet Optimization:** Implement dynamic weekend pricing for leisure routes. Deploy smaller, efficient aircraft for lower-demand weekday departures to maintain load factors above 75%[cite: 6].
